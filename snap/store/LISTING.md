### Description

```
Sound Visual turns sound into a live 3D landscape. Every bar is one slice of the spectrum: frequency runs left to right, loudness rises up, and time rolls away into the distance, so you can watch a song's bass, vocals and cymbals move as it plays.

**Listen to anything**

* Everything your computer is playing: music, videos, games, calls
* A single app on its own, such as your browser or music player
* Your microphone or line in

**True stereo**

The left channel rises above the centre and the right channel hangs below it, so you can see where sounds sit in the stereo field.

**Explore it**

* Drag to rotate, right-drag to pan and scroll to zoom
* Auto view takes a slow, random tour around the grid
* Seven colour schemes: Spectrum, Fire, Ocean, Neon, Matrix, Sunset and Mono
* Auto level keeps the loudest sounds near the top; or set the level yourself
* Full screen with F

**Allow recording**

Sound Visual needs permission to record audio. After installing, run:

    snap connect sound-visual:audio-record

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere: the sound is analysed on your computer and only drawn on screen.
```
